Miso Salmon Rice Bowls
Dinner Fish 35 min
Ingredients
Salmon fillets · white miso · maple syrup · short-grain rice · broccoli · sesame seeds · scallions
Method
Whisk miso and maple into a glaze. Roast salmon and broccoli on one sheet pan at 425° for 12–14 minutes, glazing twice. Serve over rice, finish with sesame and scallions.
